    EVAPORATIVE TECHNOLOGY
    Evaporative cooler uses evaporation to help cool the air. Based on the principles of evaporation, hot and dry outside air is pumped through water-soaked cooling pads. As the air is pushed through the cooling pads, the water evaporates and the heat in the air is absorbed, which lowers the air temperature. A fan then pushes the cool air throughout a house via a network of ducts.

    ENVIRONMENTALLY FRIENDLY
    Evaporative air conditioners use far less electricity compared to refrigerated systems, therefore, fewer fossil fuels such as coal and natural gas are consumed in the production of electricity. Evaporative air conditioning systems contain no harmful synthetic refrigerants. The power consumption of just one ducted reverse cycle air conditioner can result in up to 1.6 tonnes of harmful CO2 being pumped into the atmosphere each year.
