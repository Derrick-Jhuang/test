    TO HAVE A MORE COMFORTABLE ENVIRONMENT

    In 2004, Aeropower Ventilation Co., Ltd. was established in Taichung City, Taiwan. At the beginning of its establishment, Aeropower focused on the development and production of negative pressure exhaust fans as well as insiste on Taiwan design and Taiwan manufacturing to ensure our product quality.

    In view of the increasingly awareness global warming, we introduced water cooling technology in 1998 with environmental protection and energy saving as the main axis. We also insisted the design, development and production of related products that were promoted to livestock farms. Therefore, the sales area has expanded from the initial Taiwan to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe and United States. Since then, Aeropower has become a leading brand in the industry. In 2019, Its turnover has grown by more than 50% in Taiwan.

    CREATE A PLACE WHERE YOU CAN BREATHE INTELLIGENTLY

    With the advent of the technological age, the trend of industrial automation has gradually been importance by enterprises and factories. Aeropower has taken the lead in developing intelligent control, which has brought new breakthroughs to automation and also built ventilation system management for industrial environments.

    In the end of 2008, we actively cooperated with the industry in the field of Internet, and proposed the 4.0 concept which constitutes a complete information communication between conveying equipment and systems, and brings revolutionary changes to the industry.
